CalmCampus

A privacy-first mobile app that helps universities detect early signs of student stress and burnout before they escalate. Developed for the KU Leuven KICK Challenge 2026, where the project reached the finals with a preventive approach to student wellbeing.

The app builds a personal baseline from behavioural signals already on a student's smartphone — typing patterns, app usage, screen-on time and sleep rhythms — and on-device AI flags meaningful changes that may indicate elevated stress. All raw data stays on the device, so CalmCampus is designed to be GDPR-native and privacy-preserving.

When warning signs appear, it offers evidence-based micro-interventions such as breathing exercises, mindfulness prompts, break reminders and peer-connection nudges, with referrals to campus counselling when needed. Through a university licensing model, students use the app for free while institutions receive anonymised wellbeing insights.

Nike Poster Campaign

Three posters for a Nike campaign built around diversity in sport, designed in Photoshop for a Brand Identity and Graphic Design class. We researched Nike's existing advertising and built a mood board before designing swimming, football and tennis posters.

Each athlete is cut out and placed on a sport-specific background, both turned black and white, with a single accent colour per poster. Every poster carries "Just do it.", set in Futura PT, and each composition takes its own approach. Made entirely without generative AI.

Marketing Campaign May 2025

Immoscoop Communication Campaign

A one-week Boosterweek project in which five of us set up a fictional agency, House of Vision, and built a full communication strategy for Belgian real estate platform Immoscoop — positioning it as a "caretaker" in the home search.

We targeted "The Busy Bees" (Belgian families with young children), set three SMART objectives using the DAGMAR model, compared Immoscoop with Immoweb, Zimmo and Immovlan using Semrush data, and built a benefit ladder around its map-drawing tool. The message: "Where the growth of your family begins." The media plan combined social media, commuter-route billboards and radio.

Market Analysis Dec 2024

Engels Logistics — Marketing Environment Analysis

A Boosterweek project in a cross-class team of four, analysing the marketing environment of Engels Logistics, a European supplier of sustainable logistics and waste-management solutions. We started with an interview with the company.

The analysis covered the micro, meso (Porter's Five Forces and a 4P comparison with Sulo and Transoplast) and macro environment, CSR and the SDGs the company pursues, and ended in a SWOT. From it we proposed a sustainability-focused rebrand ("Driving Sustainability") and an educational blog with a carbon footprint calculator.
